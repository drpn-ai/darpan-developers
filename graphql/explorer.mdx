Paste your `dgql_` key into **Authorization**, choose an example in the request panel, and press **Send**. Every query goes to the same endpoint; the body's `query` says what to read.

- **Your key goes only to the API.** Your browser sends the request straight to the API host; developers.drpn.ai never receives the key. The browser remembers it for this site, so on a shared computer clear the **Authorization** field when you finish.
- **Pick the host.** **Hosted Darpan** is `https://api.drpn.ai`. For your own instance, choose **Self-hosted Darpan** and enter its backend host; it must list `https://developers.drpn.ai` in [`webapp_public_api_origins`](/self-host/production).
- **Start with Check your key.** It works on every tenant. The other examples name an audit (`month-close`), a date and a finding id (`F-20790`): replace them with ones from your tenant, or the answer is `… was not found`.
- **Write your own.** Switch the body to **JSON** and edit `query`. Field names and types are in the [reference](/graphql/queries/audit-report); the full schema is at `GET /rest/s1/graphql/sdl`.

Something refused? See [When it does not work](/graphql/quickstart#when-it-does-not-work).
