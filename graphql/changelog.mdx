## Unreleased

- `auditReport` and `auditFinding` queries: audit runs over GraphQL, in the `darpan.audit.report/v1` and `darpan.audit.finding/v1` shapes.
- `category(id:)` on the audit report and `statement(id:)` on a category: one element by id, `null` when there is none.
- `findings` query: every finding of the key's tenant, paged, with `query:` keys `audit`, `statement`, `category`, `state`, `period` and `firstFound`, sorted by `FIRST_FOUND` or `STATEMENT`.
- API keys can be rotated with a grace period, disabled, and listed by an administrator.
- Every type, field and argument in the schema has a description.
- An audit query explains an expected refusal in plain words; other failures no longer show internal detail.
